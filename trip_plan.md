## Packing List

### Clothing
- Light layers (t-shirts and long-sleeve shirts)
- Comfortable walking pants/shorts
- Light jacket or cardigan for overcast days
- Waterproof/rain jacket
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Sturdy shoes for cobblestone streets (Alfama, Sintra)
- Sun hat and sunglasses for the Sintra trip
- Light scarf or shawl for cooler evenings
- Nice outfit for Fado dinner show
- Socks and undergarments for each day

### Gear
- Compact umbrella
- Packable rain poncho
- Daypack/backpack for day trips
- Reusable water bottle
- Portable phone charger/power bank
- Crossbody bag or money belt for city walking
- Travel adapter (Type F for Portugal)
- Small dry bag for electronics in case of rain

### Toiletries
- Travel-size shampoo and conditioner
- Toothbrush and toothpaste
- Deodorant
- Sunscreen (SPF 30+) for sunny Sintra day
- Lip balm with SPF
- Basic first-aid kit (band-aids, pain reliever)
- Hand sanitizer
- Blister pads/moleskin for walking days

### Other
- Portugal travel guide or offline map app
- Reusable shopping tote for Chiado shopping
- Cash (euros) for small vendors and Ginjinha tasting
- Camera for Sintra and boat tour
- Copy of passport and travel documents
- Light packable tote for museum/market days
- Snacks for the Sintra day trip
- Laundry bag for used clothes near end of trip

_Notes: Lisbon's weather during this trip is mostly mild and overcast with a few rain/drizzle days and one clear, sunny day for the Sintra excursion. Pack breathable layers you can adjust throughout the day, a reliable rain jacket and umbrella for the scattered showers, and sturdy comfortable shoes for the city's uneven cobblestone streets and hilly viewpoints. Reserve sunscreen and a hat for the Sintra day trip, and bring a slightly dressier outfit for the Fado dinner show. Since most activities are walking-heavy with occasional indoor museum stops, prioritize comfort and versatility over bulk._
